## Traditional Diwali Celebration (10)

**T1 · Children with fuljhadi** — Three Indian children (about 6–11) on the courtyard of a lamp-lit home at night, laughing as they swirl sparklers (fuljhadi) in bright circles. Marigold torans on the door, a rangoli on the floor, rows of clay diyas along the steps, string lights on the balcony, a parent watching from the doorway.

**T2 · Safe crackers in the lane** — A happy family in a quiet residential lane at dusk. Children hold flowerpot crackers (anar) that fountain golden sparks, at a safe distance with a bucket of water nearby. Homes lit with diyas and fairy lights, colourful fireworks in the sky above the rooftops.

**T3 · Lighting diyas together** — A grandmother guides two children to light a long row of clay diyas on a home's front steps, faces glowing in candlelight. Marigold garlands, a large rangoli, brass lamps, soft evening blue sky.

**T4 · Family Lakshmi Puja** — An Indian family of five in traditional clothes (silk saree, kurta) sitting on the floor before a decorated home altar with a framed image of Goddess Lakshmi on a lotus. Brass diyas around the altar, a puja thali with kumkum, rice, sweets and flowers, marigold and mango-leaf toran, rangoli at the entrance, warm devotional light.

**T5 · A humble home, a bright Diwali** — A financially modest but joyful family outside their small, neat village home with whitewashed walls and a tiled roof. The mother and children light clay diyas along the wall, a simple chalk rangoli at the door, one string of lights, children smiling. Dignified, warm, hopeful, never pitiful.

**T6 · Diwali decoration market** — A lively traditional Indian bazaar street at evening, lined with shops selling lanterns (akash kandil), torans, garlands and lights. Crowds shopping in festive clothes, colourful hanging lanterns overhead, string lights across the street, warm glow from the shopfronts.

**T7 · Diya sellers** — A potter family's roadside stall piled with hundreds of handmade painted clay diyas. Customers choosing lamps, some lit to demonstrate, baskets of marigolds, golden late-afternoon light, a busy festive street behind.

**T8 · Women making rangoli** — Three women in sarees kneeling on a courtyard floor, completing a large intricate coloured-powder rangoli with lotus and peacock motifs. Bowls of coloured powder, diyas around the edge, a decorated doorway behind.

**T9 · Sharing sweets** — Neighbours exchanging boxes of mithai and gifts at a doorway decorated with lights and torans. Smiling children, a plate of laddoos and kaju katli, diyas on the threshold, fireworks in the night sky behind.

**T10 · Village Diwali** — A wide view of an Indian village at night: mud and brick houses outlined with hundreds of diyas, a banyan tree with string lights and birds roosting, a pond reflecting the lamps, families on rooftops, a few fireworks in a starry sky.

## Gods & Divine Art (6)

Ask for a **respectful, traditional devotional painting**, faces serene and true to classical iconography.

**D1 · Goddess Lakshmi on the lotus** — Goddess Lakshmi seated on a large pink lotus, four arms in classical pose, gold coins flowing from one hand, a red and gold saree with jewellery. Two white elephants raise their trunks on either side, a glowing golden chakra halo behind her head, a lotus pond with diyas floating.

**D2 · Lord Ganesha** — Lord Ganesha seated on an ornate throne, four arms holding modak, axe and lotus, the fourth in blessing. Rich red and gold garments, a golden crown, a circular golden halo (chakra) behind his head, marigold garlands, diyas at his feet, his mouse vahana nearby.

**D3 · Lakshmi–Ganesha together** — Goddess Lakshmi and Lord Ganesha seated side by side on lotus thrones, each with a golden halo, kalash and coins before them. Rows of brass diyas, marigold torans, deep maroon and gold palette.

**D4 · Lakshmi–Ganesha Diwali Puja altar** — A beautifully arranged Diwali puja altar with idols of Lakshmi and Ganesha on a red cloth, a silver puja thali, sweets, flowers, a kalash with mango leaves and coconut, many lit diyas, rangoli in front, soft divine glow.

**D5 · Lord Rama's return to Ayodhya** — Lord Rama, Sita and Lakshman arriving in Ayodhya at night, welcomed by citizens holding lamps. The palace and ghats lit with thousands of diyas, fireworks overhead. Grand, devotional, Diwali's origin story.

**D6 · Goddess Lakshmi's footsteps & diyas** — A temple doorway at night with alta-red Lakshmi footprints leading inside. A small shrine of Lakshmi glowing within, lotus petals scattered, brass lamps, a golden halo of light.
