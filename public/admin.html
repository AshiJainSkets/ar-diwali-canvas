Install a canvas — checklist

  1. Create the canvas above, then click Generate tracking target (compiles in ~10–60 s in this browser).
  2. Download tracking print (SVG) and print it at 100 % scale, edge-to-edge on matte canvas or matte vinyl, exactly the listed size. Stretch it on the frame (full mode) or mount the plaque at the measured position (marker mode).
  3. Light it evenly; avoid glossy lamination (glare breaks tracking).
  4. Open Calibrate on a phone, enable Peek, nudge until the gold outline sits on the canvas edge, Save.
  5. The QR is printed on the tracking print. Changing the painting here never requires reprinting.
